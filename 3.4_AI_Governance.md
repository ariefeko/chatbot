## 3.4 AI Governance

### Objective

AI Governance ensures the chatbot operates safely, responsibly, and consistently with Domain business, legal, and brand standards.

The chatbot must provide helpful answers while reducing risks such as hallucination, data exposure, unsupported medical claims, and inappropriate responses.

---

### Governance Principles

The chatbot should follow these principles:

- Answer based on official Domain knowledge
- Avoid unsupported claims
- Protect user privacy
- Do not provide medical diagnosis
- Escalate sensitive cases to Customer Service
- Keep all responses traceable
- Maintain audit logs for review and improvement

---

### Guardrails

The chatbot must apply guardrails for:

| Area | Control |
|---|---|
| Hallucination | Answer only from trusted knowledge when needed |
| Medical topics | Avoid diagnosis and recommend professional consultation |
| Product claims | Use only approved product information |
| Personal data | Mask and protect sensitive user information |
| Prompt injection | Detect and block malicious instructions |
| Low confidence | Ask clarification or escalate to human support |
| Brand safety | Keep tone aligned with Domain communication standards |

---

### Sensitive Topic Handling

The chatbot should escalate or provide safe guidance when users ask about:

- Allergy
- Vomiting
- Diarrhea
- Product complaints
- Baby/child health symptoms
- Membership account issues
- Reward redemption problems
- Personal data changes

The chatbot should not make final decisions for health-related or sensitive business cases.

---

### Response Validation

Before sending an answer, the system should validate:

```text
Is the answer grounded in official knowledge?
Is the topic sensitive?
Is the confidence score acceptable?
Does the answer contain unsupported claims?
Should this be escalated to Customer Service?
```

---

### Audit & Monitoring

The platform should log:

- User questions
- Retrieved knowledge sources
- AI responses
- Confidence score
- Escalation reason
- User feedback
- Error events
- Admin review notes

These logs should be used for quality improvement, compliance review, and model evaluation.
